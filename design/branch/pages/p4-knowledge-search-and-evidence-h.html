1. Supplier onboarding requirements

1.2 Insurance

New suppliers must provide valid commercial general liability (CGL) insurance, workers’ compensation (where applicable), and professional liability insurance as specified in the supplier onboarding requirements.

This section defines the minimum insurance coverage requirements for all new suppliers.
