4.3 Supplier Insurance Requirements

All suppliers must maintain valid and adequate insurance coverage throughout the term of the agreement. This includes general liability, professional liability, and workers’ compensation insurance, with minimum limits as specified in Appendix B.

Suppliers are required to maintain at least $1,000,000 in general liability coverage and provide a certificate of insurance before onboarding.

Failure to provide the

required insurance documentation may result in suspension of onboarding or termination of the agreement.
